About Me

My research focuses on understanding the relationship between metabolic dysfunction and brain health using multimodal neuroimaging techniques including MRI, DWI, PET, and machine learning-based data modeling.

Education

  • 2022–2026: Ph.D in Neuroscience, University of Toronto, Canada
  • 2014–2016: M.Tech, BMS College of Engineering, Bangalore
  • 2010–2014: B.E, St Joseph Engineering College, Mangalore

Research Interests

  • Signal and Image Processing
  • Functional and Structural Magnetic Resonance Imaging (f/sMRI)
  • Diffusion Weighted Imaging (DWI) MRI
  • Positron Emission Tomography (PET)
  • Abdominal Fat Quantification
  • Machine Learning and Data Modeling
  • Multimodal Data Fusion
  • Neuroscience and Neuroimaging
  • Clinical Populations such as Schizophrenia, Bipolar, and Depression
